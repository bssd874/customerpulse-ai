# CustomerPulse AI — Pitch

## One-sentence pitch

CustomerPulse AI transforms fragmented customer transactions, support interactions, and call conversations into an explainable Customer 360 risk profile and actionable Next Best Action.

## 30-second pitch

Customer churn signals are usually split across payment, support, and conversation systems. CustomerPulse AI unifies those signals in a Snowflake-ready Customer 360, identifies high-value customers whose transaction activity, service experience, and sentiment are deteriorating, and shows exactly how each signal contributes to risk. It then gives a retention team the next best action, rationale, outreach language, and business objective—with a deterministic local mode that keeps the demo fully runnable.

## 60-second pitch

Retention teams rarely lose customers because of one event. They lose them when several weak signals—declining spend, failed transactions, unresolved tickets, and frustrated calls—remain disconnected. CustomerPulse AI brings those signals together as an explainable Customer 360 and turns them into action.

Our demo follows Sarah Khan, a premium, high-lifetime-value customer. Her recent successful transaction value has fallen by more than 40%, two recent overseas purchases failed, three support issues remain open or escalated, and her latest call shows clear frustration and switching intent. CustomerPulse scores every factor transparently and places Sarah at 100 out of 100 HIGH risk.

On Snowflake, the same model is expressed in SQL, with current `AI_SENTIMENT` and `AI_COMPLETE` paths for voice insight and concise retention recommendations. Without cloud credentials, the complete pandas engine and deterministic recommendation remain available. The result is not another opaque churn score; it is an evidence-backed decision workspace that tells a retention team who to contact, why now, what to say, and what business outcome to pursue.
