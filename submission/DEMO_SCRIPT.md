# CustomerPulse AI — 90–120 Second Demo Script

## 1. Introduce the problem — 0:00–0:12

**Narration:** “Customer churn rarely begins in one system. Transaction decline sits in payments, unresolved friction sits in support, and switching intent sits inside a call transcript. CustomerPulse AI connects those signals before the relationship is lost.”

## 2. Open Portfolio Overview — 0:12–0:25

**Action:** Show the top metrics and risk-sorted watchlist.

**Narration:** “The portfolio view immediately shows our high-risk population, average risk, and—most importantly—high-value customers at risk. Every customer is sortable by value, score, sentiment, unresolved tickets, and transaction trend.”

## 3. Point out Sarah Khan — 0:25–0:32

**Action:** Locate the first row, Sarah Khan.

**Narration:** “Sarah Khan rises to the top. She is a premium customer with 248,500 dollars in lifetime value, but her risk is now 100 out of 100.”

## 4. Open Customer 360 — 0:32–0:43

**Action:** Select the Customer 360 tab; Sarah is selected by default.

**Narration:** “Instead of asking an analyst to inspect four source systems, CustomerPulse assembles Sarah’s complete relationship, value, service, and voice context on one page.”

## 5. Show transaction decline — 0:43–0:52

**Action:** Point to Relationship Value and the recent-versus-previous values.

**Narration:** “Her successful transaction value dropped by more than 40 percent across comparable 90-day windows, and two recent overseas transactions failed or were declined.”

## 6. Show unresolved tickets — 0:52–1:01

**Action:** Point to Support Health.

**Narration:** “Three issues remain open or escalated, including a card restriction and a second overseas decline after repeated contact.”

## 7. Show negative transcript — 1:01–1:10

**Action:** Point to Voice of Customer.

**Narration:** “Her latest call is strongly negative. She says the restriction is still unresolved and that she may move to another provider.”

## 8. Show explainable risk — 1:10–1:20

**Action:** Point to the four risk contributions.

**Narration:** “The score is fully auditable: 30 points for strongly negative sentiment, 25 for unresolved tickets, 25 for transaction decline, and 20 for repeated failures.”

## 9. Show explanation and Next Best Action — 1:20–1:40

**Action:** Open Next Best Action.

**Narration:** “CustomerPulse explains the combined risk and recommends a P1 response: a senior retention specialist within 24 hours, end-to-end ownership of the blocker, suggested outreach, and the objective of restoring confidence. In this environment the output is honestly labeled as deterministic; with Snowflake AI access the same evidence is sent to current AI_COMPLETE.”

## 10. Ask Customer Data — 1:40–1:53

**Action:** Open Ask Customer Data and submit “Who should the retention team contact first?”

**Narration:** “A controlled question layer turns the same governed data into a grounded retention queue—without open-ended SQL generation or invented values.”

## 11. Close — 1:53–2:00

**Narration:** “CustomerPulse AI turns fragmented signals into one explainable decision: who is at risk, why, and what the business should do next.”
